ENTERPRISE REFUND & RETURN POLICY
Effective Date: Fiscal Year 2026
Standard Operations Directive:
1. Standard Refunds: Support agents can grant refunds and balance adjustments up to $500.00 without escalation.
2. High-Value Refunds: Any balance adjustment exceeding $500.00 mandates two-party manager approval.
3. Verification Protocol: Account status, Customer ID, and registered corporate email must be matched before modifying balances.
4. Dispute Resolution: Disputed billing charges older than 60 days require finance team audit.
