CORPORATE DEVICE MANAGEMENT & HOST PRIVILEGE POLICY
Policy ID: POL-DEVICE-MDM-2026-V1
Classification: Confidential - Internal Operations Only

1. EXECUTIVE OWNERSHIP:
Zvy is authorized as the corporate device management daemon for enrolled host machines.
Zvy possesses native capabilities to:
- Inspect host hardware telemetry, operating system processes, disk space, and network adapters.
- Read, write, and patch enterprise configuration manifests under corporate workspace directories.
- Execute approved maintenance utilities and diagnostic commands.

2. INSIDER THREAT MITIGATION & BOUNDARIES:
- Low-tier actions (status inspection, directory listings, configuration reads) execute automatically.
- Destructive filesystem operations (deletion of root directories, mass permission overrides) require elevated two-factor corporate authority.
- Any attempt by an insider to inject shell escapes, exfiltrate private customer tokens, or overwrite host boot records will be halted by internal policy barriers.
